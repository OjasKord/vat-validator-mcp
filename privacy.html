What we collect

VAT numbers you submit are never logged or stored. Query content — the VAT numbers, company names, and invoice amounts passed to our tools — is transmitted to the relevant government API and immediately discarded. It does not touch our database.

Where data is stored

Subscriber API keys and rate-limiting counters are stored in Upstash Redis (hosted in the United States). The application itself runs on Railway (hosted in the United States). Both providers maintain industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit.

We do not operate our own database servers. No data is stored in Singapore.

Third-party services

When you call our tools, your VAT number is forwarded to the relevant official government registry to perform the lookup. These are read-only API calls — no personal data about you is sent to them.

Legal basis and UK GDPR

Where we process personal data relating to individuals in the UK or EEA, we do so under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — processing your email and API key to deliver the service you subscribed to.
  • Legitimate interests — rate limiting by truncated IP address to prevent abuse of the free tier.
  • Legal obligation — retaining transaction records as required for tax compliance.

We comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. For EEA residents, we comply with EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679). Transfers of personal data to the United States (Upstash, Railway, Anthropic) are made under Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent adequacy mechanisms.

Data retention

  • API keys and associated email addresses are retained for the lifetime of the subscription plus 90 days, then deleted.
  • Truncated IP rate-limit counters reset automatically each calendar month.
  • Aggregate tool usage statistics (tool name + timestamp, no content) are retained for up to 12 months for service improvement.
  • Stripe retains payment records in accordance with their own privacy policy and applicable financial regulations.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase personal data we hold about you. You also have the right to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights — including requesting deletion of your API key and associated email — contact us at:

Email ojas@kordagencies.com

If you are located in the UK, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. If you are in the EEA, you may contact your local supervisory authority.

Cookies and tracking

The VAT Validator MCP API does not use cookies, browser tracking, analytics scripts, or fingerprinting of any kind. This privacy policy page itself does not set any cookies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised. Material changes will be communicated to active subscribers by email.
